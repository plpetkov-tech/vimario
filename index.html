Three stars: every coin, no death, keys at or under par. Holding one key dies once the pits start. u undoes one motion. The key still counts.

Thirty courses

  1. 1 Mushroom PlainsWalk, then w as a shortcut, then a vault with a treasure detour, then a small king. About ten minutes.
  2. 2 Word HillsRed shelves, a lava paragraph, a toll that moves the exit, then a coin behind you.
  3. 3 Line CaveLine ends, a find you can repeat, letters over red shelves, then an exit behind you.
  4. 4 Insert CastleFind the real line, open a locked door, then a king whose nearest letter is a lie.
  5. 5 Match MarshW skips commas. % jumps to the matching bracket. b runs the course backward.
  6. 6 Edit OutpostWrite the missing exit, hunt a repeated word, then jump the blank lines.
  7. 7 Case LibraryCapitals burn. guu quiets a line. gUw shouts one word so you can walk it.
  8. 8 Mark Hallma drops a pin. 'a jumps home. . repeats the last delete.
  9. 9 Chord KeepA loud word you can leap or quiet, a bracket and a step, then a lock behind a letter that burns.

Fuse is on the title menu. One motion, then a shorter window. A miss ends the run. Room 12 pays a bonus, then overtime. A new card after midnight.

Original game. Side-view courses in the spirit of NES platformers. Buffer rooms in the spirit of Vim Adventures.
